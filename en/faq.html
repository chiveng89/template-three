What products can I ask Menglong about?

We focus on aluminium doors and windows. Our installations include sliding doors, folding windows and hinged doors and windows. Share the style you need and ask the team to confirm current options.

How do I request a quotation?

Call 099 881 199, message our Facebook page, or use the enquiry builder on the contact page to prepare an email. Include the product type, project location, approximate dimensions and any useful photos or drawings.

Can I arrange measurement and installation?

Contact the team to discuss site measurement and installation. Share your project location and timing so Menglong can confirm the arrangements.

Do you work outside Phnom Penh?

Menglong is based in Phnom Penh and has completed an installation in Koh Reah, Lvea Em. For other locations, contact the team to confirm availability and any travel arrangements.

What is the price of a door or window?

Ask for a quotation for your specific opening. Dimensions, frame, glass, hardware and installation requirements need to be agreed before the price can be confirmed.

What glass, colours and hardware are available?

Send your preferred style or reference photo to the team. Menglong will need to confirm current glass specifications, frame finishes and hardware options for your order.

How long does an installation take?

Confirm the schedule with Menglong when discussing your project. Product availability, the size of the order and site readiness can affect the arrangements.

Where can I visit Menglong?

Find us at No. 261K, San Han Street, Toul Sangke 1, Russey Keo, Phnom Penh. Please call 099 881 199 before travelling to confirm opening hours and that someone can meet you.

Can I ask about repairs or warranty terms?

Yes. For a repair enquiry, send photos and describe the issue so the team can confirm whether it can help. Ask Menglong directly about warranty and aftercare terms for your particular order.
