All questions & answers
How do I request a quotation?

Call 099 881 199 or send us your project details through email or Messenger. Include the location, approximate dimensions and the door or window style you have in mind.

Can you help with measurement and installation?

Yes. Contact our team to discuss site measurement and installation arrangements for your project.

Where can I visit Menglong?

Find us at No. 261K, San Han Street, Toul Sangke 1, Russey Keo, Phnom Penh. Please call before visiting to confirm opening hours.
