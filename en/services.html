Planning your enquiry

Start with these three things.

A little information helps us understand your space and discuss a suitable door or window.

01

Your space

Tell us where the project is and whether you need doors, windows or both. Include photos of the openings if you have them.

02

Your preferred style

Share a sliding, folding or hinged style you like, together with rough dimensions or a building plan.

03

Your project timing

Let the team know when you need the work. Confirm measurement, quotation and installation arrangements directly.

Before work begins

The details matter.

Ask the team to confirm the frame finish, glass specification, opening direction, hardware, price and installation schedule for your order.

For an existing door or window that needs attention, send photos and describe the issue so Menglong can confirm whether it can help.
